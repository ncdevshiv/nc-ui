- The frame wire format gained version 2: custom islands — display lists with clips, text runs, blits and hit regions — now cross a process boundary. Encoding picks the smallest version the content needs (a solid-only frame still travels as v1, so a v1 peer never sees bytes it cannot parse); decoding dispatches on the version byte and rebuilds the command stream through the display list's own builder, so a malformed stream fails exactly the validation a mistaken producer would — unknown tags and role codes, non-UTF-8 strings, unbalanced clips and runaway counts are all refused rather than walked. The v1-era `CustomContentNotEncodable` refusal is gone: the frame the producer built is the frame that travels. `crates/ncui-gpu-renderer/src/wire_v2.rs` with seven tests, including truncation refused at every byte position and the public `Frame::encode`/`decode` dispatch seam. What remains for `gpu.island_custom` is rendering, not transport: text runs and bitmap blits still refuse loudly in `RenderPlan` until the glyph-atlas and texture-binding passes land.
