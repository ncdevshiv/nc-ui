Goal: $ARGUMENTS

Work like this:

1. Restate the goal in one sentence and list acceptance criteria.
2. Inspect the repository before proposing edits.
3. Propose the smallest complete change: numbered steps, files to touch, risks, and what you will not do.
4. Wait for approval before editing. Do not edit until the user says go.
5. After approval, execute step by step, running the relevant tests after each change.
6. Finish with a short summary: changed, skipped, and remaining risk.

If $ARGUMENTS is empty, ask what the goal is.
