- **A consent prompt no longer parks the runtime while the person reads it.** `consent::resolve` used to block on the human dialog while holding `&mut Runtime` — the runtime lock in the shared-transport topology — so an unanswered prompt froze every other caller for the answer's whole life, and an agent-transport prompt the human never sees (no parent, no prompter on a connection thread) froze the whole app indefinitely. Consent is now two-phase on both hostage sites: `consent::gate` judges the call under a brief lock and returns either a dispatch decision or a `ConsentGate::Ask` carrying the request and an owned prompt surface; the person is asked with no lock held at all; then the caller re-enters under the lock and `consent::settle` turns the answer into a minted `ConsentToken` or the identical `consent_denied` error and audit. The agent transport runs the three phases in `serve_request`; the page path parks the call as `PendingPageConsent`, asks on a worker thread, and `pump_pages` resumes the parked call through the full pipeline — rate limit, capability check, audit — under the id the page is waiting on, bounded to eight parks and a 120-second patience. Regression evidence proves the lock is free mid-prompt on both surfaces (`try_lock` succeeds and an unrelated call is served while the person decides; approve and decline both settle to the right caller). The page-dispatch half of `window.rs` moved into `window/page_dispatch.rs` under the file-size cap.
