- `media.video_surface` acceptance measured through the full runtime path, and the measurement first caught a real architectural defect: the frame path shared the runtime's lock with the page pump, costing a 1080p30 stream p99 63ms against its 33ms frame budget in mutex convoy. `Runtime::video_surface_producer()` now hands producers a cloneable registry handle — the only exclusion a frame waits on is the one serializing presentation against teardown. Re-measured at ten minutes sustained (release): **1080p30 proven** — 18,000 frames in exactly 600.0s, p50 3.25ms / p99 5.50ms push-to-presented, zero drops, zero cadence lateness, no degradation, scrub-to-presented 3.20ms. **4K30 honestly not met**: the rate held with zero drops but p99 35.36ms broke budget with real degradation (11→21ms over ten minutes); the 33MB-per-frame memcpy churn is the named suspect with buffer pooling the named fix direction.
