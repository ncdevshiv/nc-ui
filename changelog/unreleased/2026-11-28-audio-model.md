- `media.audio_output` first increment: the host-agnostic audio model in `ncui_gpu::audio` — stream identity, the 48kHz stereo float32 canonical format, and a bounded sample ring whose semantics invert the video ring's deliberately: audio never drops (a dropped block is a click, where a dropped frame is a mercy), so overruns are counted refusals and underruns are counted silence. Timeline positions track as anchor-plus-exact-frame-count — at 90kHz over 48kHz the per-sample increment is never integer, so the design divides once at query time off exact integers rather than rounding drift into the position truth. Seven tests pin the semantics including the no-drift proof at ragged callback sizes. The z-order-after-resize question is answered with negative evidence: WebView2 does not re-raise its child on host resize, and `video_surface_composite_live.rs` now pins that permanently.
